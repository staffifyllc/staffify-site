Partnership questions.

What does Tier 2 mean here?

A supporting staffing or delivery relationship with an existing supplier or contract holder. It does not imply that Staffify holds the end-client contract or is already approved by a particular enterprise or MSP.

Can you work under our delivery model?

We assess your requirements together, including account boundaries, reporting, screening and onboarding. Specific white-label arrangements, integrations and service levels need to be agreed for the engagement.

What if we need a team for our own organization?

Our Enterprise Workforce Solutions page covers direct team-building discussions. For individual hires, explore our core staffing services.
