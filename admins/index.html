How we find the right admin for you

An admin lives inside your business. We can't just send a resume and call it done. Every Staffify executive admin runs through the same process so the person sitting in your inbox actually sounds like you, thinks like you, and protects your time the way you would.

Before we touch a candidate, we walk through your inbox, calendar, CRM, and project tracker with you. We document the repetitive workflows, the recurring decisions, and the "quick things" that aren't quick. That becomes the spec we hire against.

Onboarding plan modeled upfront

We track how long each test task actually took, so by the time we recommend someone, you know not just how they perform but how much of your weekly admin load they can realistically absorb. You see the load-out before you commit.

How to evaluate an executive assistant before hiring

Give candidates the same sanitized inbox scenario, calendar conflict and incomplete CRM record. Ask them to explain what they would do, what they would leave untouched and when they would escalate. Compare prioritization, clarity and attention to detail against a rubric you agree on before reviewing candidates.

A useful first-month review

Track missed handoffs, calendar corrections and the time you still spend reviewing work. Record the starting point before the assistant joins; hours assigned are not automatically hours saved.

Need someone focused on customer tickets? Compare the support role.Discuss your role and screening criteria

What should your executive assistant own?

A practical weekly role

Start with recurring work you can define: triage email, prepare calendar briefs, update CRM records, follow up on missing documents, and coordinate approved appointments. Give each task an owner, deadline, and definition of done.

Decisions that stay with you

You approve spending, contracts, sensitive replies, and changes to scope. Your assistant flags exceptions and prepares the context for your decision. Start with limited access and expand responsibility as the work is reviewed.

A practical hiring and handoff checklist

  1. List recurring tasks for one week, including frequency and time spent. Separate coordination from specialist work.
  2. Bring a sanitized email thread, calendar conflict, and CRM example to the role discussion. Define the response you would accept for each.
  3. Agree on business hours, response targets, escalation rules, and a daily handoff before the assistant starts.
  4. Review completed work together, record corrections in the SOP, and measure your own remaining review time.

Questions to settle before hiring

What does a virtual executive assistant do?

A virtual executive assistant handles recurring administrative work remotely: inbox triage, scheduling, CRM updates, document follow-up, and client or vendor coordination. The agreed role determines which tasks and decisions they can own.

How much does a Staffify executive assistant cost?

Staffify publishes a base range of $11.25–$14 per hour depending on the role, plus a small one-time onboarding investment. At an illustrative $12.50 per hour and 40 hours per week, the annualized monthly average is about $2,167 before onboarding. Confirm your role-specific quote and agreed hours.

Can an assistant cover every hour of the day?

One full-time assistant covers the agreed working schedule, not every hour of the day. Define time-zone overlap, breaks, backup coverage, and urgent escalation before assigning customer-facing work.

Should I hire an assistant or a customer service rep?

Choose an executive assistant for varied coordination across your business. Choose a customer service rep when the main workload is a sustained ticket, chat, or phone queue with support policies and service targets.
