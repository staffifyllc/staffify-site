Before you hire: agree on four things

  1. Work: the recurring tasks, weekly volume and definition of done.
  2. Coverage: working hours, time-zone overlap and who handles absences.
  3. Authority: tool access, approval limits and escalation owner.
  4. Review: work samples, feedback rhythm and the outcome you will measure.

Questions about hiring virtual assistants

What is a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant is a person who works remotely on agreed business tasks. Staffify recruits dedicated remote professionals for executive support, customer service, sales support and video editing. The role, working hours and responsibilities are agreed before the placement starts.

Which type of virtual assistant should I hire?

Start with the work that repeats each week. An executive assistant handles scheduling and coordination; a customer service rep handles a support queue; a sales assistant supports approved prospecting and follow-up; a video editor handles production. Choose the role around the workload rather than expecting one person to do every specialty.

What should I prepare before hiring a virtual assistant?

Bring a list of recurring tasks, examples of acceptable work, required tools and time-zone overlap. Identify who will approve sensitive decisions and review early work. Staffify uses that information to scope the role and screen candidates.

Can Staffify help us build a team rather than hire one assistant?

Yes. For multiple roles, define the work streams, team leads, coverage and quality checks together. Staffify offers a separate enterprise workforce discussion for organizations building larger teams.
