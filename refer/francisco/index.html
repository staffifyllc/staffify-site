For Francisco

What to say, and what to send.

Two businesses, two different kinds of owner. You do not need to pitch either one. All you are doing is spotting the problem in conversation and offering to make an introduction. Paul does the selling.

Start here

The only thing you have to do

"Hey, I know a guy who does this. Want me to connect you?"

That is the whole job. Do not explain pricing, do not describe how it works, and do not try to answer their questions. Every question they ask you is a reason to get them on a call with Paul instead. The two scripts below are only for recognizing which of the two businesses fits.

The two businesses

Staffify

Full-time assistants, placed inside a business

Say this

"You know how you're still doing all the admin yourself? Paul's company places a full-time assistant inside businesses like yours. Not a freelancer you have to manage, an actual person who works your hours and only your account."

"Want me to introduce you? Costs you nothing to hear it."

Who this is for

  • Service businesses doing roughly $500K to $5M
  • The owner is still doing the admin: inbox, scheduling, invoicing, follow-up
  • They have said some version of "I need to hire but I can't justify a full salary"
  • They tried Upwork or Fiverr and it did not stick
  • Real estate, agencies, law firms, med spas, dental, ecommerce, coaching

Send them this

gostaffify.com/cost Best opener. Answers the price question before they ask it.
gostaffify.com/case-studies For the skeptic who wants proof.
gostaffify.com If they just want to look you up.
Book a call directly Send this only when they have already said yes.
Why the cost page first: the first question every owner asks is what it runs. Sending the answer up front makes you look like you know the business, and it filters out anyone who was never going to buy.
Foundry

Websites, built rather than templated

Say this

"Paul also builds websites. Before you pay him anything he'll run a free audit and show you exactly what's costing you leads on the one you have now."

"Want me to have him send it over? No charge and no pitch, you just get the audit."

Who this is for

  • Local businesses whose site is old, slow, or clearly a template
  • Anyone who says "our website is embarrassing" or "we get nothing from it"
  • They are spending on ads but sending that traffic to a weak site
  • Med spas, dentists, clinics, contractors, home services

Send them this

There is nothing to send yet. Foundry has no website of its own, so the free audit is the entire offer. Get the name and the URL of their current site to Paul and he sends the audit.
The audit is the hook, not the pitch. You are not asking anyone to buy a website. You are offering a free look at what is wrong with the one they already paid for. Almost nobody says no to that, which is exactly why it works.

The handoff

How to actually pass one over

01
Get permission first "Can I give Paul your number?" A warm name beats a cold link every single time.
02
Text Paul four things Name, business, phone, and one line on what they said. For a Foundry lead, add their current website address.
03
Or make it a group text You, them, Paul, one message: "Meet Paul, he's who I mentioned." This converts better than anything else on this page. Then you are out.
04
Stop there Do not chase it and do not follow up on price. Paul takes it from the introduction.
Questions on any of this, or you have someone and you are not sure which one they fit, just text Paul.
