The queue

It remembers.

The last outcome decides where each person goes next. Callbacks someone asked for come first when they are due. Then "not now" leads whose time has come. Then people nobody has called yet, best numbers first.

  • Didn't pick up? They wait at the back, fewest tries first.
  • Wrong number? The lead goes looking for a better one.
  • Not interested stops all outreach to that person.
  • The top bar shows callbacks due, new and to try again.

The call card

One person. One call.

The dialer hands you one prospect at a time. Who they are. Their local time, and whether it's business hours there. The number, and where it came from. A talk track that branches with every reply.

  • Objection handling, ready when the conversation turns.
  • Seven outcomes on number keys 1 to 7.
  • Notes, the next step and tasks on the same card.

Honesty

No flattering numbers.

Only the phone system's record counts as a verified call. There is no "picked up" rate, because voicemail and phone menus pick up too. Anything the Hub could not read says unknown.

  • A blank never passes for a zero.
  • Activity is never turned into payroll.
  • Callers see their own work. Owners see the team.
