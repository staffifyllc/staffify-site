Before

Founder-carried outbound that broke every busy week.

The sales motion ran the way most agency sales motions run for the first few years. Hand-built lists. Cold calls between client deliverables. LinkedIn messages drafted one at a time. Follow-ups on Post-it notes.

  • Every outbound week reset. Lists went stale, follow-ups got skipped, and nothing accumulated.
  • Founders were the bottleneck. Busy production weeks killed outbound. Pipeline emptied 60 to 90 days later.
  • Hiring an SDR didn't pencil. $5,500 to $9,000 a month loaded for a US SDR was a non-starter at the agency's stage.

After

An outbound system that runs whether the founders are at a shoot or not.

Staffify deployed verified prospect lists, multi-step sequenced cadences (day 3, day 7, day 14 follow-ups that actually go out), and reply-first routing that surfaces warm replies to the founders within hours.

  • The volume job, automated. List building, deliverability, sequenced sending, follow-ups. The system runs whether anyone is at a desk.
