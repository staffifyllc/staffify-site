How much does an executive assistant cost?

Separate the hourly rate, the hours you need, and one-time setup. Here is a worked Staffify budget and a checklist for comparing it with a direct hire or part-time service.

By Staffify · Updated September 22, 2026

Quick answer

Staffify publishes a base rate of $11.25–$14 per hour, depending on the role, plus $2,499 in one-time onboarding. At 40 hours per week, that is about $1,950–$2,427 per month before onboarding, using 52 weeks divided by 12 months. Your written quote and actual hours determine your bill.

These are calculations using Staffify’s published pricing, not an independent market salary survey. The middle column uses $12.50 per hour as an example, not a promise that every role is available at that rate.

What is included, and what should you confirm?

Staffify’s published hourly offer includes payroll, benefits, HR support, time tracking, ongoing training, and the replacement guarantee. Onboarding covers sourcing, screening, role scoping, and workflow preparation. Confirm your assistant’s level, agreed schedule, software seats, any specialist requirements, and the full written scope before starting.

For a concrete role description, see virtual executive assistant responsibilities and the hiring checklist. An inbox-and-calendar role is different from senior executive decision support; scope the work before comparing prices.

Pay onboarding upfront or defer it

The published deferred options add $1 or $2 per hour until the $2,499 onboarding balance is repaid. That is 2,499 worked hours at $1, or 1,249.5 worked hours at $2. At 40 hours per week those are approximately 62.5 or 31.2 weeks. Fewer hours extend the repayment period.

Deferral changes payment timing, not the onboarding cost. If you cancel before paying the balance, the remainder is due under the published arrangement. Do not add both the full onboarding fee and the repayments when calculating the total.

Compare the same work across hiring models

  • Direct employee: budget the actual salary offer plus applicable employer costs, equipment, software, recruiting, and management time. Use current local quotes rather than comparing salary alone with an agency invoice.
  • Independent freelancer: confirm availability, minimum hours, responsibilities, replacement coverage, and who manages the work. A low hourly price does not tell you how much supervision a task needs.
  • Part-time service: compare included hours, overages, scheduling, and whether the same person owns the work. This can fit a smaller recurring workload.
  • Dedicated Staffify placement: compare the role-specific rate, realistic hours, onboarding, included support, and your own remaining review time.

Workload comes before headcount

Track a week of inbox, calendar, CRM, document follow-up, and coordination tasks. Record frequency, duration, tools, and decisions that still require you. If the work does not justify a full-time role, improve the process or discuss a smaller scope first. Do not assume an assistant’s hours translate directly into the same number of owner-hours saved.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Staffify executive assistant cost per month?

At the published $11.25–$14 hourly range and an assumed 40-hour week for 52 weeks, ongoing staffing averages about $1,950–$2,427 per month. One-time onboarding is $2,499. The written role-specific quote and actual hours determine billing.

What is the first-year cost at $12.50 per hour?

At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, staffing is $26,000. Adding $2,499 onboarding gives $28,499 for the first year, or about $2,375 per month as an annualized equivalent. This is a budgeting illustration.

Is onboarding included in the monthly staffing example?

No. Ongoing monthly staffing excludes the one-time $2,499 onboarding. The first-year total includes it once. Deferred onboarding adds repayments to invoices until the balance is paid.

How much does an in-house executive assistant cost?

Use a current salary quote for the role and location, then include employer costs, equipment, software, recruiting, and management time. This page does not present an independent market salary survey.

What changes the price of an executive assistant?

Seniority, scope, working hours, specialist skills, tools, and coverage needs affect the quote. Compare the same responsibilities and schedule across options.
