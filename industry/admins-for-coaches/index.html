You are doing the work that pays (calls with clients, hosting the group, recording the podcast) and then doing all the work that does not pay (provisioning Kajabi access, scrubbing the ConvertKit list, drafting the welcome email, chasing the podcast guest who never confirmed, repurposing the episode into three social posts, and replying to the same five DMs you reply to every week). Course platform admin (Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific)

New student provisioning, course module updates, drip release setup, certificate generation, refund processing inside your refund window, and student support tickets answered with your approved language. Cohort launches set up end-to-end from your launch checklist.

Client onboarding and offboarding

List hygiene (ConvertKit, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign)

Podcast outreach and guest coordination

Guest outreach drafted in your voice, calendar booked, intake form sent, pre-call brief assembled, post-call thank-you and asset-share email sent, and the episode metadata updated everywhere it lives (Apple, Spotify, YouTube, your website). Long-form content (podcast episode, webinar, YouTube video) sliced into short-form posts, quote graphics built in Canva from your template, captions drafted in your voice, and scheduling queued in Buffer, Later, or Metricool for your review before publishing.

Inbound DMs, comments, and email

Tier-1 inbound across Instagram, LinkedIn, email, and your community handled with your approved replies. Sales-intent or coaching-fit conversations flagged to you with context. The coach replies to the right messages, the admin handles the rest.

Onboarding cadence.

Most coaches have an admin running onboarding, the list, and podcast coordination inside 21 days. Week 1: SOP build (your client onboarding flow, your course access SOP, your email voice samples, your content repurposing checklist). Week 2: paired work, the VA drafts and you review every client-facing message and every social post. Week 3: solo on tier-1 with daily review of a flag list. By month 2 the coach has reclaimed the morning admin block. See the hidden cost of doing it yourself for the math on coach admin time.

Where this works (and where it does not).

Strong fit: coaches at $15K-plus MRR with an active email list, a course or membership, and a content engine (podcast, YouTube, newsletter). Particularly strong for coaches running cohort-based programs where launch periods spike admin work. Also strong for consultants running group programs or masterminds with recurring billing.

Weaker fit: brand-new coaches under $5K MRR (you do not yet have full-time work), and high-end advisory consultants whose entire workflow is one-on-one and bespoke (the repeatable admin layer is thinner). See first employee vs VA for the decision framework.

We replace them. Lifetime replacement guarantee, no extra fee. Most replacements happen in the first 30 days and are usually about voice or pace fit, not skill.
