Virtual assistants for real estate agents and teams

A dedicated assistant can keep CRM records, listing preparation, document follow-up, and scheduling moving while agents own client advice and decisions.

By Staffify · Updated September 22, 2026

Quick answer

Start with the recurring administrative work: lead routing, CRM updates, appointment coordination, listing files, and deadline tracking. Define coverage hours, approval rules, and permitted system access before assigning the work.

What a real estate virtual assistant can own

CRM follow-up

Update records in your approved CRM, assign leads, log contact attempts, schedule approved follow-ups, and flag conversations that need an agent. Define what qualifies a lead for a handoff; do not ask an assistant to improvise advice or make commitments for you.

Listing preparation

Collect photos and property information, organize files, prepare listing drafts and approved social posts, and check that agent details and links are correct. An authorized agent reviews property claims and approves publication.

Transaction administration

Track the checklist you provide, follow up on missing documents, coordinate appointments, and flag approaching deadlines. The assistant supports administration; the responsible professional retains interpretation, advice, and decisions.

A daily handoff you can use

  • New inquiries: source, contact status, assigned owner, and next approved action.
  • Appointments: confirmations, unresolved scheduling conflicts, and information the agent needs.
  • Active files: checklist item, due date, missing input, and person responsible.
  • Exceptions: the decision needed from the agent, relevant context, and deadline.
  • Completed work: links to updated records so the agent can review without searching.

Use a shared queue rather than personal message threads. Keep only the information needed for the task and follow your brokerage’s access and review requirements.

Test the role with sample work

Give candidates a sanitized CRM record, a scheduling conflict, and an incomplete listing packet. Ask them to identify missing information, draft a follow-up, and escalate decisions outside the checklist. Score completeness, accuracy, clarity, and judgment before speed.

For tools such as Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, DocuSign, or your listing platform, confirm the candidate’s experience and permitted access. A tool list is a scoping requirement, not a guarantee that every candidate knows every system.

Choose the right role and workload

An executive assistant fits administrative coordination. A sales assistant fits a defined prospecting and appointment-setting workflow. An editor handles video production. Separate these needs before trying to put them into one job.

If you run a photography or media company, the media workflow map focuses on file intake, revision tracking, and delivery rather than agent transaction support.

Frequently asked questions

What does a real estate virtual assistant do?

A real estate virtual assistant can support CRM updates, approved lead follow-up, scheduling, listing preparation, document collection, and checklist tracking. Agents retain advice, approvals, and decisions outside the administrative role.

Can one assistant handle administration and sales?

Only if the combined workload, skills, and schedule fit. Separate administrative coordination from prospecting targets and closing responsibilities when scoping the role.

What do I need before onboarding a real estate VA?

Prepare your task list, approved scripts, CRM access rules, listing checklist, working hours, escalation contacts, and sample work with unnecessary personal information removed.
