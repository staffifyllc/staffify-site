5 BELAY alternatives: compare the model before the price.

Reviewed September 22, 2026 · Written by Staffify
Start with your hours, required schedule, and candidate fit. The lowest hourly rate and the lowest total monthly cost are not always the same choice.
This guide is published by Staffify, which is one of the options below. The order is not an independent quality ranking.

BELAY describes U.S.-based assistants and ongoing support. Before switching, ask your current provider whether a scope, schedule, or matching adjustment would solve the problem. If you compare alternatives, send each one the same role brief.

1. Staffify

For a dedicated remote role in the Philippines or Latin America. Compare the full-time schedule and standard onboarding cost with the hours you need. Staffify pricing and terms.

2. Magic

For a dedicated assistant with coaching and a 24/7 support product; request current pricing and coverage details. Magic official information.

3. Wing

For a managed general-assistant plan with a published monthly price; verify the scope for specialist work. Wing official information.

4. Boldly

For experienced assistance based in North America or Europe; compare monthly hours and candidate fit. Boldly official information.

5. Time Etc

For a small monthly allocation of U.S. assistant hours; compare the published plans with your task list. Time Etc official information.

Budget for the complete arrangement

Staffify’s published base rates are $11.25–$14 per hour, with standard one-time onboarding of $2,499. At $12.50/hour and 40 hours/week, using 52 weeks ÷ 12 months, ongoing cost is about $2,167/month. Including standard onboarding makes the first-year monthly equivalent about $2,375. Actual hours, role, payment options, and any volume terms belong in your quote.

A small hours plan can cost less overall than a full-time placement even when its hourly rate is higher. Include onboarding, expected additional hours, required software, handoff time, and cancellation terms in your comparison. Do not assume that “dedicated” means the same weekly availability on every plan.

Plan the handoff

Document recurring tasks and deadlines, name an owner for questions, and agree on the first week’s deliverables. Confirm approved access to your tools and how access will be removed when an arrangement ends. Candidate location alone does not settle security or procurement requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Which BELAY alternative should I choose?

Match your actual hours, candidate requirements, location, and budget to a written quote. Staffify, Time Etc, Magic, Wing, and Boldly offer different arrangements; there is no single best option for every workload.

How much can I save by switching?

A savings calculation needs your current total cost and a comparable new quote, including onboarding and hours. We do not promise a fixed percentage.

Can a fractional assistant learn my business?

Yes. Continuity, clear documentation, and regular feedback matter. A smaller hours plan does not by itself establish the quality of the assistant or their knowledge of your business.
