The 5 best Belay alternatives, by what you're solving for.

Last updated July 2026 · 7-minute read
Quick answer The best Belay alternative depends on why you're leaving. If the per-hour premium is the problem, Staffify places a dedicated full-time assistant at $11.25-14/hr all-in — typically 40 to 60% less for far more hours. If you want lighter commitment, Time Etc (hourly bundles) or Magic (on-demand) fit. Wing is the budget offshore entry, and Boldly is the premium US-based upgrade.
Disclosure: this guide is published by Staffify. We're the first alternative below because dedicated full-time is our category — and we say plainly when Belay or another service is the better call.

Belay is a legitimate, well-run service — most people shopping for alternatives aren't leaving because it's bad. They leave for one of three reasons: the US-based price premium stops making sense past 20 hours a week, they've outgrown fractional attention and want one person all-in on their business, or they want faster scaling without renegotiating a plan. Match your reason to the alternative below.

2. Time Etc — for lighter hours without US premium plans

Time Etc

Best hourly bundles
Monthly hour bundles · US/UK assistants

If Belay's engagement model feels heavy for the 5 to 20 hours a month you actually use, Time Etc's hour bundles are the simpler shape: buy hours, use hours, scale up or down. Assistants remain US/UK-based, so the cultural-context argument holds.

Watch for: the same fractional-attention ceiling as Belay — nobody on an hourly bundle deeply learns your business. Comparison: Staffify vs Time Etc.

3. Wing — for the lowest managed-offshore entry price

Wing

Best budget entry
Offshore subscription plans, entry tiers well below US services

Coming from Belay, Wing's appeal is price: managed offshore assistants on a subscription with an app-based workflow. A reasonable way to test offshore delegation cheaply before committing to a dedicated hire.

Watch for: attention pooling on lighter plans and shallower screening than a dedicated placement. Comparison: Staffify vs Wing.

4. Boldly — for going more premium, not less

Boldly

Best premium upgrade
Premium subscription · W2 staff, US/EU

Some people leave Belay upward: they want deeper executive experience and employee-grade continuity. Boldly's W2 model (long-tenured US/EU staff) is that upgrade, priced accordingly.

Watch for: the highest per-hour cost on this page — roughly triple a dedicated offshore hire. Comparison: Staffify vs Boldly.

5. Magic — for zero-commitment task overflow

Magic

Best on-demand
Request-based, on-demand · no commitment

If the honest answer is that you don't have recurring assistant work, don't replace Belay with another subscription. Magic's on-demand model handles sporadic tasks with zero onboarding.

Watch for: request-based pricing compounds fast if usage becomes daily. Comparison: Staffify vs Magic.

The decision in one sentence.

Count your real weekly hours: under 5, go on-demand (Magic); 5 to 20 a month, hourly bundles (Time Etc); testing offshore cheaply, Wing; want premium US continuity, Boldly; 20-plus hours a week of steady work, a dedicated full-time placement (Staffify) delivers the lowest cost per productive hour and the only model where one person compounds knowledge of your business.

Common questions.

Why do people switch away from Belay?

Three patterns: the US-based per-hour premium stops making sense past ~20 hours a week, fractional attention caps how much context the assistant builds, and scaling hours requires plan changes. None of these are quality complaints — they're model limits.

How much cheaper is a dedicated offshore VA than Belay?

Typically 40 to 60% less per hour for full-time dedicated help. Staffify runs $11.25 to $14 per hour all-in (~$2,167/month full-time) including payroll, HR, training, monitoring, and replacement. US-based fractional services generally run $35 to $65 per hour.

Is switching hard if I already have processes documented?

Documented processes make it faster. Staffify trains your new assistant on your existing SOPs and tools before day one, and typical time-to-first-billable-hour is 14 to 20 days.
