Staffify vs Wing: two offshore VA models, compared.

Wing Assistant runs a platform-led, app-managed VA model. Staffify runs a hands-on staffing model with deeper training and tighter pairing. Here is the trade off.

Last updated June 2026 · 7-minute read
The quick verdict Both place offshore talent. Wing wins on entry price for light, task-based plans. Staffify wins on dedication and depth: your VA works for you alone (never pooled), is screened from roughly 200 applicants down to 3 finalists you interview, and carries a lifetime replacement guarantee with month-to-month terms.

The short version

Pick Wing if you want the lowest published full-time price, app-based task management, and you are comfortable owning the training and SOP work yourself.

Pick Staffify if you want a VA paired and trained on your tone and SOPs, deeper hand-holding on onboarding, and a lifetime replacement guarantee instead of a free swap window.

Who Wing Assistant is, accurately.

Wing Assistant (wingassistant.com) is an offshore VA platform that publishes pricing openly: roughly $699 per month for a part-time VA (80 hours) and $999 per month for a full-time VA (160 hours). Wing markets itself as a managed execution platform with a proprietary Workspace App that handles tasking, monitoring, and quality control, plus a customer success manager assigned to your account.

Wing offers 25-plus role types (general VA, executive assistant, SDR, CSR, bookkeeping, healthcare, real estate, and more) and includes free replacement, supervisor oversight, and SOC 2 compliance. Specialist roles run roughly 20 to 40 percent above the general VA rate.

Who Staffify is, accurately.

Staffify pairs you with a full-time dedicated virtual assistant based in the Philippines or Latin America. One VA, one client, paired and trained on your tone and your standard operating procedures. We focus on service businesses (agencies, real estate, home services, e-commerce, coaching, consultancies).

Typical pricing is $2,000 to $4,500 per month for a full-time placement, varying by role and seniority. Roles we staff most often: Executive Admins, Video Editors, Customer Service Reps, and Sales and Outbound. Every placement is backed by a lifetime replacement guarantee.

When Staffify is the better choice.

Wing's headline number ($999 per month full-time) is genuinely lower than ours. We will not pretend otherwise. The trade off is in what comes around the VA.

Where Staffify tends to win is when the work has nuance: tone of voice, your specific reply patterns, an idiosyncratic SOP for handling leads, a complicated CRM hand-off. We do more hands-on pairing and training upfront, which costs more, and our clients tend to be founders who want their VA to feel like a teammate, not a contractor running tickets through an app.

Read more on offshore talent fit and SOPs that stick if you are evaluating how deeply you want the VA to be embedded in the business.

When Wing is the better choice.

Wing is the right pick when:

  • You want the lowest possible monthly cost for an offshore dedicated VA and the published $999 full-time rate fits your budget.
  • You prefer an app-based, ticket-style workflow over a relationship-style one. Wing's Workspace App is designed around managed task flow.
  • You have well-documented work already, so the VA does not need much white-glove training to be productive on day one.
  • You need SOC 2, HIPAA, or ISO 27001 compliance posture for a regulated workflow.

Pricing reality check.

On sticker price, Wing wins. $999 per month full-time is cheaper than a $2,500 to $3,500 Staffify placement.

What you are paying the difference for, with us, is the time spent pairing and training the VA on your business in the first 30 days, ongoing performance management, and the lifetime guarantee that we will keep replacing the placement if it stops working for any reason. That is a real cost on our side. Whether it is worth it for you depends on how much your time is worth and how complex the work is. If it is not, Wing is a reasonable choice.

Frequently asked questions.

Is Wing Assistant worth it?

Yes, for buyers who want low-priced offshore VAs with app-managed task flow and are comfortable owning more of the training and management themselves. If you want deeper pairing and trained-on-your-SOPs onboarding, a higher-touch service is usually a better fit.

How much does Wing Assistant cost?

Wing publishes pricing at roughly $699 per month for a part-time VA (80 hours) and $999 per month for a full-time VA (160 hours). Specialist roles (SDR, healthcare, real estate, etc.) cost 20 to 40 percent more.

Are Wing VAs dedicated?

Yes, each Wing plan comes with a dedicated VA. The platform layer (Workspace App, customer success manager) sits around the VA to handle quality control and oversight.

What is different about Staffify training?

We spend more time upfront pairing the VA to your business: tone, SOPs, inbox cadence, tool stack, escalation patterns. That is part of why our placements run higher than Wing's published rate.

Where are Staffify VAs located?

Philippines and Latin America. Time zone coverage depends on the role.
