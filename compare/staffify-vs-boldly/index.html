Which arrangement fits your workload?

A founder who needs a few scheduled hours of experienced executive support has a different requirement from a team filling a full daily inbox or support shift. Compare relevant candidate experience and the actual work schedule. Location alone does not establish whether a service meets your security, procurement, or regulatory requirements.

Write down the recurring tasks, expected weekly hours, required availability, and who approves the work. Ask each provider to price that same brief. Check what happens when the workload increases, the assistant is away, or the match needs to change. A lower rate is useful only if the available hours and proposed skills cover the job.
