Staffify vs Boldly: premium US/UK staffing vs dedicated offshore.

Boldly is one of the highest-priced premium VA subscriptions in the category. Staffify is full-time dedicated offshore. Here is the honest trade off.

Last updated June 2026 · 7-minute read

The short version

Pick Boldly if you want a North America or Europe-based VA with 10-plus years of experience as a W-2 employee of an established staffing firm, and the premium price ($2,600 to $6,500 per month for 40 to 100 hours) fits your P&L.

Pick Staffify if you want a full-time dedicated offshore VA at a similar or lower monthly cost, with 160 hours per month of capacity and a lifetime replacement guarantee.

Who Boldly is, accurately.

Boldly (boldly.com) is a premium subscription staffing firm placing dedicated VAs based in North America or Europe. They publish pricing openly: $2,600 per month for 40 hours, $3,900 for 60, $5,200 for 80, $6,500 for 100, with enterprise plans up to 160 hours. Specialist rates run $79 per hour for project management, web development, writing, and design tasks outside core EA work.

Boldly's talent claim is "10 to 15 years of experience" and the VAs are W-2 employees of Boldly, not contractors. They include a one hour launch meeting, a backup VA for vacation or sick coverage, and a 100 percent satisfaction guarantee with monthly refunds if unsatisfied. No long term contracts, ACH preferred (credit cards add 2.9 percent).

If you want a senior, US or UK-based, employed VA who plays well in regulated or executive-facing environments and you can carry the price tag, Boldly is one of the strongest options in the premium tier.

Who Staffify is, accurately.

Staffify pairs you with a full-time dedicated virtual assistant based in the Philippines or Latin America. One VA, one client, trained on your tone and SOPs. Typical pricing is $2,000 to $4,500 per month for a full-time placement.

Roles we staff most often: Executive Admins, Video Editors, Customer Service Reps, and Sales and Outbound. Every placement is backed by a lifetime replacement guarantee.

When Staffify is the better choice.

The dollar comparison is striking when you normalize on hours. Boldly's 80 hour plan is $5,200 per month, which works out to $65 per hour. A Staffify full-time placement at $3,000 per month for 160 hours is about $19 per hour. You are buying twice the hours at roughly a third of the hourly rate.

If your bottleneck is bandwidth (the work won't stop, you need a teammate, not 10 hours per week of senior support), the math heavily favors a dedicated offshore VA. The same is true if the work is operational rather than strategic: inbox triage, CRM hygiene, video editing, CSR coverage, outbound prospecting.

When Boldly is the better choice.

Boldly is the right pick when:

  • You need a senior, US or UK-based assistant with a long professional resume, often for executive support to a CEO or partner with high-stakes scheduling and stakeholder management.
  • You require W-2 employment status from your staffing partner (procurement, compliance, or insurance reasons).
  • Voice and accent matter: client calls with US enterprise contacts, board prep, investor coordination.
  • You only need 40 to 100 hours per month and the per-hour premium for senior US/UK talent is acceptable.

For that buyer profile, Boldly is one of the best-run premium services on the market. We will say it plainly: if you are a Fortune 500 exec who wants a 10-year experienced EA in your time zone, Boldly is a more natural fit than Staffify.

Pricing reality check.

Boldly is a premium product priced like one. $65 per hour for senior US/UK W-2 talent is honest pricing for what you get. The question is whether your business needs the seniority and zip code, or whether it needs more bandwidth at a lower cost per hour.

Most service businesses we talk to need more capacity, not more seniority. A trained full-time VA who owns the inbox 8 hours a day usually outperforms a senior 20 hour per week W-2 EA on operational throughput. If your need is strategic chief-of-staff work, that flips.
