Before you choose.

How should I compare Staffify and BELAY?

Use the same role brief for both: responsibilities, required hours, location, tools and outcomes. Compare the complete proposal, including onboarding, ongoing fees and replacement terms.

Is Staffify less expensive than BELAY?

The answer depends on the actual proposals and coverage. We do not claim a savings percentage without equivalent scopes and current quotes.

Does BELAY offer replacement support?

BELAY publishes a Right-Fit Guarantee with rematching at no additional cost when the original match is not aligned. Staffify provides covered replacements without a new onboarding fee while you work with us. Review each provider’s written terms.

Can I start with one person and build a team?

Yes. Staffify can scope an individual placement first, then plan additional roles around your workload. Team size, management responsibilities and coverage are agreed in your proposal.
