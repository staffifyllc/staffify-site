Questions before building a team.

How are workforce programs priced?

Pricing follows the roles, team size, locations, working hours and support model. We discuss these requirements before proposing commercial terms.

Can we start with a smaller team?

Yes. Discuss a phased approach with defined roles and review points. A small initial team can establish the workflows before a larger rollout is considered.

Do you support offshore, nearshore and contingent arrangements?

These are part of our workforce scoping discussions. The available sourcing locations and engagement model depend on your roles, requirements and delivery feasibility.

Start with your requirements.

Discuss your workforce needs

Share your company, the work you need covered and the team you have in mind. If known, include locations, working hours and timing. We will discuss scope, delivery fit and the next step before making a staffing commitment.
