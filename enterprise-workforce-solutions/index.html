Questions worth asking

Get the details before you decide.

Can we start small before building a larger team?

Discuss a phased start with defined responsibilities, onboarding needs and review points. Team size and timing depend on the work and available capacity. Expansion should follow a review of delivery and management readiness.

Who manages the team day to day?

That must be defined in your scope. Recruiting, payroll or HR support does not automatically mean Staffify owns your daily operation. We discuss client supervision, any team-lead role, quality ownership and escalation before deployment.

What will our team need to provide?

A business owner, priorities, access approvals, examples of the work and subject-matter input for training. The onboarding plan identifies these dependencies so responsibilities are clear on both sides.

How are pricing and replacement arrangements set?

Pricing depends on roles, location, coverage, headcount and support scope. Review setup and recurring costs, inclusions and exclusions in your proposal. Replacement responsibilities and terms are agreed for the program; individual-placement terms do not automatically apply.

How do you approach access and sensitive information?

Bring your security, confidentiality and procurement requirements to scoping. Supported controls, access responsibilities and any contractual requirements must be reviewed before system access or a delivery commitment.

Can Staffify support our existing staffing contracts?

We welcome discussions with staffing firms, MSPs and established enterprise suppliers that need additional sourcing or delivery capacity. Explore staffing partnerships to discuss scope, candidate ownership and supplier responsibilities.
