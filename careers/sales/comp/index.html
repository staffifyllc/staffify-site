The Money

Commission per close. Residuals that stack.

This is a 1099 contractor role and the structure is in writing before you take a single call. You earn five ways: a commission on every onboarding you close, a residual on every hour your placements bill for their first 6 months, more commission when your clients grow, a higher rate on your strong months, and a bonus when your placements stick. No caps anywhere, so your income comes down to how much you sell and how well you grow and keep your accounts.

Where This Goes

A starting line, not a ceiling.

Closing is where you start, not where you stop. Produce, and there is a real path from running your own pipeline to running the entire revenue engine. Not everyone makes this climb. Every level is earned on results, and the higher you go, the more of the team's output you share in.

  • Independent Sales Agent. Where you start: your own pipeline, closing placements, commission only and uncapped. Clear $120k in your first year and the Sales Manager track opens up.
  • Sales Manager. Lead a team of closers and take the larger, strategic deals yourself. Base salary plus your own commissions, an override on everything your team closes, and team bonuses.
  • Sales Director. Own every team, the hiring, and the sales strategy. Six-figure base plus an override on the whole org's output plus revenue bonuses.
  • Director of Revenue. Run the entire revenue engine: new sales, account expansion, and retention. Base plus variable tied to total revenue growth.

The exact numbers at each level are shared privately once you're on the team. The path is opportunistic and earned, but it's real. We would rather promote a proven closer than hire a manager from outside.
