The Role

What you'd actually be doing.

  • Run discovery calls with warm inbound leads from our site, ads, and referral channels.
  • Close placements: a one-time onboarding fee (upfront or financed) plus an ongoing hourly engagement per VA placed.
  • Work your own pipeline too — outbound is welcomed and paid the same. LinkedIn, referrals, your network.
  • Hand off after the close. Sourcing, screening, onboarding, HR — our team runs all of it. You stay in your lane: selling.
  • Use the toolkit we've built: live pitch deck, role landing pages, real candidate profiles, case studies, pricing calculator.
A Day in the Life

Warm leads to work, and a phone to pick up.

Let's be straight about the job so you can decide if it's yours. This is a real sales seat. Some days you're closing warm leads we hand you. Other days you're building your own pipeline from scratch. The best people here do both.

  • Warm leads from Staffify. Inbound from our site, ads, and referral channels lands in your pipeline. Real business owners who already raised their hand.
  • You'll also hunt. This role includes outbound. Cold calls, networking, and reaching out to business owners to share what we do. If picking up the phone to a stranger makes you flinch, this is not your seat.
  • Run the presentation. You deliver a full sales presentation remotely over video: walk the owner through the offer, handle the objections, and close the placement.
  • Own your day. Your follow-ups, your calendar, your numbers. 1099 means nobody counts your hours, only your results.
  • Close and hand off. The moment it's signed, our team takes sourcing, screening, onboarding, and HR. You go back to selling.

The warm leads are real and they keep coming. But the top earners here don't wait on their inbox. They network, they prospect, and they share the opportunity everywhere they go. Come ready to build.

Training Provided

You won't be thrown in cold.

1099 doesn't mean unsupported. Before you take live pipeline, we train you on the whole operation:

  • The offer, inside out: pricing, the full onboarding breakdown, both payment paths, and the lifetime replacement guarantee, so you can answer anything without checking notes.
  • Call recordings + scripts — real discovery calls that closed, the talk tracks that work, and objection handling for the ones that come up every time.
  • Mock-call reps with us — you practice on our team before you're ever in front of a prospect.
  • The full sales toolkit — live pitch deck, candidate profiles, case studies, and role landing pages, ready to share on any call.
  • Ongoing — call reviews and feedback as you ramp. When you win, we study why. When you lose one, same.
