How to evaluate a sales support candidate

Use a sample account brief and an approved offer. Ask the candidate to explain fit, draft a short relevant opener and record the next step in a sample CRM. Evaluate research accuracy, respectful follow-up and qualification judgment before increasing activity.

A useful first-month review

Separate outreach activity, replies, scheduled calls, attended qualified calls and opportunities. Define disqualifiers and the owner of each handoff so a busy calendar does not hide poor fit.

Need CRM upkeep without outbound prospecting? Explore executive support.Discuss your role and screening criteria

Define a qualified meeting before building the funnel

Work a sales VA can own

Research prospects against your customer profile, maintain contact records, work approved follow-ups, classify replies, arrange meetings, and keep next steps current in the CRM. Define permitted channels and review messaging before launch.

Work your closer still owns

Discovery, pricing exceptions, proposals, negotiation, and closing stay with the person you authorize. A meeting is useful only when the prospect matches your criteria and understands why the conversation is happening.

A practical hiring and handoff checklist

  1. Define your offer, ideal customer, disqualifiers, and the evidence needed before a meeting is booked.
  2. Approve the list criteria, messages, outreach permissions, suppression rules, and channel limits.
  3. Test the reply-to-booking handoff: context, qualification notes, time zone, and the next-step owner.
  4. Track attended qualified meetings and opportunities alongside activity. Keep cancellations, no-shows, and unqualified bookings separate.

Questions to settle before hiring

What does a sales virtual assistant do?

A sales virtual assistant supports prospect research, approved outreach, reply handling, follow-up, appointment setting, and CRM updates. The role supports your sales process; closing and commercial commitments remain with your authorized team.

Is appointment setting the same as closing sales?

No. Appointment setting creates a scheduled conversation with a prospect who meets your agreed criteria. A booking is not an attended meeting, a sales opportunity, or revenue.

Does Staffify provide the workflow as well as the VA?

The sales offer includes scoping outbound infrastructure and a dedicated operator. Confirm the channels, setup work, software, data costs, staffing rate, and ongoing management in your written proposal.

Are meeting volumes guaranteed?

There is no fixed meeting-volume promise on this page. Results depend on your offer, audience, contact permissions, channel performance, capacity, and follow-through. Agree on measurement before launch.
