The Role

What you'll actually do.

You are a closer for Staffify. We place dedicated, top-1% virtual assistants inside US service businesses, and your job is to take a call and turn it into a placement. You sell. Our team runs everything after the signature.

  • Run discovery calls on warm leads we hand you, and on the pipeline you build yourself.
  • Close placements: a one-time onboarding fee plus an ongoing hourly engagement per VA placed.
  • Source your own deals too. Outbound is welcomed and it pays you more (see below).
  • Hand off after the close. Sourcing, screening, onboarding, HR, all ours. You stay selling.
  • Build your team as you ramp. By day 60 you begin hiring and training closers under you.

How You're Paid

Base is the floor. Commission is the climb.

On top of your weekly base, you earn a commission on every onboarding you close, plus a residual on every hour your placements work. Your rate depends on where the lead came from, and it climbs as you produce.

Where the lead came fromYour commission
From Paul's network. The warm leads we hand you. 25%then 35% after your 4th close
Sourced by you. Any deal where you generated the lead yourself. 35%from the very first one

On a standard $2,499 onboarding, 25% is about $625 and 35% is about $875 per placement. Financed and volume deals adjust the base, and your percentage holds. There is no cap on how many you close.

How it pays out. Your base is paid weekly. Your commissions and residuals are paid bi-weekly, and always paid when paid: you earn on a deal once the client has paid Staffify for it. It keeps us on the same side of the table, every time.

Residuals that stack
$1 per hour, every VA you place, for 6 months.

Every hour a VA you placed works pays you $1, from that VA's start date, paid to you bi-weekly. It runs for 6 months per hire, then drops off. A full-time placement is about $173 a month, and every active placement stacks on top of the last.

Team bonus
$750 when the team places 10 VAs in a month.

When your team lands 10 new VAs inside a single month, you earn a $750 bonus. It is a team number, earned together, and it is one more reason to build a strong bench under you.
